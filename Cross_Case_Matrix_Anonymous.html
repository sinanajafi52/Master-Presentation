📊 Cross-Case Pattern Summary: The tables above reveal that organizational agility in startups is shaped by an interplay of team size, developmental stage, sector regulation, founder backgrounds, and resource availability. While all seven organizations embrace agile values (trust, learning, empowerment), they implement these values through diverse structural mechanisms and practices tailored to their specific contexts.

💡 Research Implications: This cross-case matrix demonstrates that (1) organizational agility is context-dependent rather than universal, (2) effective agile practice requires principled customization based on deep understanding rather than mechanical framework application, (3) cultural foundations prove more important than structural arrangements, and (4) hybrid models combining agile and traditional elements represent pragmatic necessity rather than failure.
